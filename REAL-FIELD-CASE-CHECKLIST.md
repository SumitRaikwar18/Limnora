# Real field case checklist

Use this before recording the final demo. Do not fabricate or reuse generated images as field evidence.

## Needed evidence

- Original permissioned photograph from a real freshwater body.
- Actual observed date and time.
- Water body selected on the map, or explicitly declared as unmapped freshwater.
- Observer category.
- Water-body kind: pond, lake, stream, river, reservoir, wetland, or unknown.
- Water movement: still, flowing, dry, or unknown.
- Visible water colour.
- Visible clarity.
- Bank litter.
- Aquatic life.
- Naturally noticed odour from the bank, or unknown.
- Visible coverage estimate.
- Optional second visit or revisit photo.
- Explicit permission to show the image in the demo and Devpost submission.

## Safety

- Stay on public accessible land.
- Do not enter the water.
- Do not touch suspicious water or plants.
- Avoid photographing identifiable bystanders.
- Do not disturb animals or habitat.
- Record Unknown rather than guessing.

## Demo note

If no genuine revisit exists, show the pending revisit task honestly. Do not imply improvement, worsening, or completed follow-up without evidence.
